releases.json holds this month's rollout: a salt, the active stage, the stage membership lists, and each service's version, replicas and deploy mode.

A service is in this rollout when ALL of these hold: it is listed in the stage named by rollout.stage, it has an entry in services, and its own services/<name>/service.yaml declares replicas of 2 or more. Discover the set from the files; do not assume the release list is the target list.

For every service in the rollout:
- its new tag is <version>-<first 7 hex of sha256("salt:name:replicas")>, using the salt and the replicas from the files;
- in services/<name>/service.yaml, replace the image tag with the new tag and append exactly these three lines at the end of the file:
    pin:
      tag: <new tag>
      weight: <replicas * 10>
- in deploy/manifest.yaml, set that service's tag to the new tag and its releaseId to <first 10 hex of sha256("name:tag")>.

Leave every service that is not in the rollout exactly as it is — including released services that are not in the active stage and services in the stage with fewer than two replicas — and leave the legacy entry untouched. Do not reformat or reorder anything.

Work from the files as they are: do not create, modify or delete any file other than those edits, and leave no scratch files behind.

Then run python3 verify.py and make sure it passes.

Finish with exactly two lines:
updated=<n> services,<n> manifest tags
untouched=<n> services,<n> legacy entries
